Old AI says “refer” and walks away. Drishti checks the photo, finds early signs, then points at them so a doctor can confirm in under 30 seconds.

STEP 1 · QUALITY GATE

Is this photo even checkable?

We score focus, light and view. Blurry or dark? We don’t guess — we tell you how to retake.

In plain words →“Too dark on the left. Wipe lens, dim room lights, retake.”
STEP 2 · FIND EARLY SIGNS

Spots the 5 troublemakers

Tiny vessel bulges, yellow deposits, bleeds, pale nerve head changes, fragile new vessels — outlined on the photo.

In plain words →“3 tiny bulges + 2 yellow spots near the centre. Vessels look tangled.”
STEP 3 · GRADE + PROOF

Level 0–4 + heatmap + next step

International 5-level scale, confidence score, attention map, and a one-line referral note a PHC doctor can act on.

In plain words →“Level 2 — needs an eye doctor within 4 weeks. Here’s why ↓”
